GoodTrip Settings Codes.

Every GoodTrip AIO setup fits in a short code: the mod shows yours on its Misc tab and writes it at the top of its save file. Paste a code or open a save to see the settings, change any of them right here, share a link, get a save file, or compare two setups.

Where the save is: in the game's folder, data/goodtrip_aio/save1.dat (save2 and save3 for the other save slots). On Steam that is Steam/steamapps/common/The Binding of Isaac Rebirth/data/goodtrip_aio/save1.dat. Close the game before replacing it.

In the game: open the console and type gtimport and the code; gtcode prints your own.

Searches every tab: names, descriptions, choices. Press / to start typing, Esc to clear.

Generated from the mod's own settings list, so labels and defaults match the menus. Codes from a newer version show what this page knows and skip the rest. Pages, doodles and lettering: the Dead Sea Scrolls menu art the mod ships with.
